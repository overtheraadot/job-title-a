One job title, 60 characters at most and nothing else, for make.fun's
"Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown" bounty ($5 total, 1st $1.54).

The title:

> Chief Synergy Alchemist, Dept. of Meetings About Meetings

The angle is a plausible corporate title with one absurd department: a
Chief Synergy Alchemist sounds like a real C-suite job, until you learn
his whole department exists only to hold meetings about meetings.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

The program writes the single job title to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
